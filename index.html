About

Clinical depth.
Startup speed.

Most people in healthcare data have either domain knowledge or technical skills. I've spent the last three years building both — running statistical models on multi-year patient cohorts at NYU Langone, managing clinical databases at UCSF, documenting encounters as a medical scribe, and responding to 911 calls as a certified EMT.

I know what a busy clinician actually needs from a tool. I know where data collection breaks down in practice. I'm looking for an early-stage environment where I can wear multiple hats: digging into data, talking to clinical stakeholders, helping shape product direction, and doing whatever it takes to push things forward. I'm a fast learner, a strong communicator, and I'll outwork anyone in the room.
